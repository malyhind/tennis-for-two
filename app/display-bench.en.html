Reconstructed network

The archival drawing supplies an astable multivibrator, differentiator, bistable divider, drivers and six analog keys. All 15 transistors are nonlinear PNP devices; switching arises in the circuit.

The standalone bench uses separate +12, +6, −6 and −22 V sources. Six inputs are test voltages; 60 Hz sweeps exercise the keys. Bench oscilloscope loading is 1 MΩ ∥ 30 pF.

Bounded mode retains the same network: 0.12 s window, maximum 50 µs step, reltol 10⁻⁴. Reference: 0.24 s, maximum 20 µs, reltol 10⁻⁶. The short run supports BF 50–100 and temperature 10–40 °C. Frequency, edge and settled X/Y errors are checked separately in the report.

Accuracy and model choices

RCA SC-11 gives typical small-signal gain 75 for 2N140 at one operating point. Remaining Ge SPICE parameters are preliminary and affect frequency and transfer distortion. This is a working network transcription with declared device models.

Transformer, rectifier, sweep-source internals and CRT physics are outside this standalone bench. The game can pass frozen U7/U2 outputs through the six transistor keys with the net/court test sweeps.
